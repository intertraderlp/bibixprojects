3 · Keyboard shortcuts (inside the chat)

Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + EnterTranslate the draft and send it
Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + TTranslate the draft in place (review, then press Enter yourself)
Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + UUndo: restore the original draft

4 · Quick answers

Ready-made replies, organised in groups. In the chat, click ⚡ in the toolbar (or Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Q): one click puts the answer in the message box so you can edit it (Enter then translates & sends), double-click translates it to the chat's language and sends it right away. Write answers in your own language.
